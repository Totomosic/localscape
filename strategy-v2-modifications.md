# Strategy v2

In general most of the concepts remain the same just a couple of gear changes and priority assignments pre-cap have been adjusted. There are no blert recordings for this strategy.

## Ranger adjustments

### Gear adjustments

Swap toxic blowpipe for ascension crossbows.

Swap eye of ayak for zorya's tome.

### Pre-cap

On wave 3 you can use the extra range of the ascension crossbows to hit the wave 3 south range small instead of chinning it. This is useful generally, the extra range is very useful for certain waves.

Do not claw special attack the wave 6 melee big, the mage will kill it. Instead get the wave 7 mage small south and the wave 6 mage small west.

On wave 11, you will kill the west mage 11 big with a zorya's tome special attack, then focus any rangers or mages the mage is not hitting. You have a lot more fire power now with the zorya's tome and can single handedly prevent stalls in pre-cap with it.

Aim to use the tome special attack twice during pre-cap. Once on the wave 11 big and once any other time from wave 7 onwards where there are a lot of mages. Almost any time you are maging you should be special attacking or using the stored 2 tick attacks.

### Post-cap

Wave 24 is the biggest change, start by killing the wave 24 west big. If you are ahead you can ascension crossbow it twice before it switches style, or you can wait for it to flicker to mage and zorya's tome special attack it. Immediately after, use the zorya's tome to kill the wave 24 south mage big (special attack if you haven't already).

Overall you will use 3 zorya's tome special attacks (2 in pre-cap and one at wave 24).

## Mage adjustments

No gear adjustments.

### Pre-cap

Instead of hitting the west wave 6 mage small in the lane you are going to hit a split from the w5 range big and then go and scythe the wave 6 east big as it enters the room. If you are early you can also hit the wave 7 small behind it but try not to be too late running across the room to his the wave 8 big.

At wave 11 you will barrage east as normal and then prioritise any new east melee smalls and then any south 11 mages (prioritise the west one so it doesn't affect the wave 12 big's position on the pillar). Let the ranger kill the wave 11 west mage big.

If you are far ahead you can kill the wave 13 big before finishing wave 15 west smalls and can kill the wave 17 mage before it enters the room (only 1 attack, ONLY ATTACK THIS IF AHEAD, it is not necessary at all).

### Post-cap

In most cases do a delayed wave 22 dinhs, hitting the east 22 big agro when it gets close. This gives you more time to kill mage smalls before the dinhs. Let the ranger kill the wave 24 mage big south and instead kill the 23 south if the ranger didn't and kill small melees. This should allow you to be very far ahead of the 24-25 big melees, you can kill them very early with the extra time save from not killing the 24 mage south.

If you get what's called an "insta-29", which means 0 wave 28 stalls before 29 spawns, then instead of doing the standard "26 dinhs" you can delay it and dinhs the wave 29 south melee big as it enters the room.
