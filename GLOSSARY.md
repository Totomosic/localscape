# Nylocas

Terms used when reviewing and training the ranger and mage roles.

## Language

**Dim**:
A birth-time cutoff anchored to a wave's actual spawn, optionally plus a tick offset: nylos born on or before that instant are ignored, while later splits remain fresh even if the same wave is still current. `29 dim` uses wave 29's spawn instant; `28+16 dim` uses 16 ticks after wave 28's spawn, the same instant when there are exactly three wave-28 stalls.

Other established room terms are listed in the [training brief's glossary](nylo-training.md#glossary).
