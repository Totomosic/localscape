# Mage strategy

## Gear setup

### Melee gear

Torva helm, Oathplate chest and legs, amulet of blood fury, ferocious gloves, lightbearer, sulphur blades, infernal cape, avernic treads (max), dragon arrows

### Range gear

Torva helm, Oathplate chest and legs, amulet of blood fury, ferocious gloves, lightbearer, toxic blowpipe, infernal cape, avernic treads (max), dragon arrows

### Mage gear

Ancestral hat, top and legs, occult amulet, confliction gauntlets, lightbearer, eye of ayak, infernal cape, avernic treads (max), dragon arrows

### Additional items

- Tumeken's shadow: used for a high damage slow weapon with long projectile delay, rarely used

- Scythe of vitur: used for better odds of killing big nylocas and attacking multiple small nylocas in a single hit

- Ancient sceptre: used for increased ice/blood barrage damage

## Basic overview

Prioritise nylocas in this order: melee, mage, range. In pre-cap you are assigned to kill the following bigs: wave 4 south mage, wave 8 west mage, wave 11 west mage, wave 13 east melee (delayed), all 19 mage bigs, 20 south mage big.

In general try not to lose ticks, but it is ok to lose ticks to be on time to key waves.

## Key waves

- Wave 4: ensure the mage big dies as soon as possible

- Wave 8: try and hit the mage big as soon as possible and kill it

- Wave 11: be 1st or 2nd tick to barraging east so that the nylos die before the next wave check

- Wave 12: you MUST scythe the wave east 12 melees first possible tick and then immediately run west and dinh's special attack the west wave 12 agro melee.

- Wave 21: you MUST barrage west first tick and then sulphur blade a melee small so that if it is overkilled it will die before the next wave check

- Wave 28: you must dinh's special attack the wave 26 big (that will be melee) right as the wave 28 west smalls enter the room so that they die before the next wave check. (this is playing for 2 wave 28 stalls)
