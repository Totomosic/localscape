# Nylocas bot partners

Create a solo lobby with `::theatrecreate`, then use `::nylobot range` or `::nylobot mage` before starting. The bot occupies a normal player slot, joins the raid's two-person roster and follows the host from the Bloat exit through the confirmed Nylocas entry. The lobby reserves its second slot and rejects additional guests. Existing started raids and shared lobbies cannot receive a bot. Live partners drink five supported finite prep doses and finish Spellbook Swap to Arceuus before entry; the entrance waits for that preparation. They summon their thralls after crossing into the arena, alongside normal movement and attacks without an added wait.

To watch a bot duo, use `::nylobot spectate` in your solo lobby before starting. This adds one ranger and one mage and reserves the lobby for you and both bots. Start the raid and pass through the Bloat exit to the Nylocas bridge. After both bots finish their potions and Spellbook Swap, they enter automatically and summon their thralls inside the arena while moving to their opening positions; you stay outside. The Nylocas barrier prevents the spectator from entering. You receive the raid HUD, wave reports and completion message; encounter scaling counts the two bots. Your inventory and equipment remain under your control. Both bots share the duo strategy controller and continue through the boss; both leave when the encounter finishes, either bot dies or disconnects, or you leave the viewing area/instance or log out.

The host controls their own player normally. Only the requested bot role is equipped, moved and given prayer/attack inputs. Its policy observes the host's actual attacks to avoid doubling targets. Multiple independent lobbies have isolated room observations and sessions.

The controller and role policies are shared with the headless server simulations. Live inputs queue the same normal NPC-operation, movement and interface-button handlers until the player's input-processing phase; the simulation adapter advances the engine before finishing each observation. Gear switches use normal inventory/equipment transactions, including two-handed conflicts and charge-bearing items. The finite loadout is provisioned once, with up to 28 inventory slots before initial equipment, charge fields initialized to 10,000, ammunition, rune stacks and four prayer potions. Switching never manufactures replacement weapons, ammunition or charges. Underlying weapon consumption follows the same server implementations as human players; the current server treats several powered weapons and blowpipe supplies as unlimited. The controller does not add a separate refill mechanism. The fixed sets are the user's range/mage specifications, including Oathplate, blood fury and confliction gauntlets. After initial equipment, a partner starting at 1–99 HP receives five manta rays in the freed inventory slots. It eats at 65 HP or below through the normal held-item handler, with ordinary three-tick bite and attack delays. The explicit 250 HP benchmark receives no food, even after later damage. The bot starts with base/current combat levels 99, ordinary finite run energy and normal combat/resource consumption; lobby options can still apply their explicit raid-level resource changes. Live prep consumes ordinary super attack/strength/defence, ranging and magic potion doses. The existing world-wide LoginScript then maintains 118 melee stats and 112 ranged/magic for bots and humans alike; this behavior predates the bot controller. Potion-only isolated tests reach 103 Magic and omit that world profile.

Training metrics end at boss spawn. Live partners then continue through a basic boss phase: claws/scythe for melee, Eye of Ayak for magic and bow/blowpipe for ranged, with matching protection/offensive prayers and normal cooldowns. Spawning form 8354 is not attacked; queued clicks are discarded if the visible form changes before input processing. Projectiles already in flight remain subject to normal impact-time style validation and reflection. This boss policy is functional rather than optimized, so deaths remain possible. The bot leaves when the room ends, a participant dies, the host leaves the room/instance or logs out, or the server shuts down.

Bots have transient identities and no persisted accounts. Explicit disposal closes queued inputs, emits the normal logout cleanup event and removes their registry/zone entry. The engine's exceptional logout path also immediately removes a bot with `saveAccountOnLogout=false`, bypassing account saving; regular players retain the default account-save behavior. One world-scoped service dispatches combat events, so creating and removing companions does not accumulate event subscriptions.

Verification covers lobby eligibility/reservation, normal command dispatch, unchanged human inventory/equipment, finite bot provisioning, immediate transient logout and host-departure cleanup. Compare simulation timing after controller changes because finite gear swaps and ordinary run energy can differ from older fixtures that fabricated worn objects every tick.

The simulation uses `nylocasSupplyScripts()` for both its batch fixture and consumable contract tests. This loads `HeldOpScript` as well as `ConsumablesScript`: without the inventory-to-held-item bridge, queued food and potion clicks never reach their handlers. The regression verifies an actual prayer dose (20→51 prayer; four doses→three) and native prep boosts (118 melee stats, 112 ranged, 103 magic). The fixture also advances one initial engine cycle before prep clicks: a newly registered player has not completed its first processing cycle and cannot yet use protected held-item actions. Earlier timing runs that omitted the bridge did not actually consume prayer or prep potions and cannot be described as boosted runs.

Native finite-food tests exercise the actual controller provisioning/support path and verify five initial manta rays, 22 HP healing, item consumption, the ordinary three-tick bite/attack delay and unchanged human inventory. Exhausted food is never replaced. Corrected 99 HP baselines retained active prayer and run energy yet still died to repeated 15–35 damage bursts, which motivates this finite survival supply.

Native utilities use a greater skeleton for range and a greater ghost for mage, with Death Charge. Initial summoning happens inside the arena; an expired thrall does not itself trigger Spellbook Swap. Players start on Lunar and use the actual Spellbook Swap component operations; spell scripts consume runes, enforce the three-tick swap, summon/attack and restore special energy on genuine kills. No book is rewritten during combat. Mage barrage preparation also uses native swaps to Ancients.

Utility supplies are finite ordinary stacks and a Book of the dead. Mage replaces its water stack with Steam runes, which natively cover barrage water and thrall fire; Book, Cosmic, Soul, Astral and Law fill its five remaining slots. Ranger carries Fire, Blood, Death, Cosmic, Soul, Astral and Law plus the book. No rune pouch is supplied because this server currently treats every pouch as unlimited. Ordinary inventory transactions and rune consumption remain authoritative.

Live utility support defaults on through `NylocasBotFeatures`; isolated control tests bind it off. Simulation runs enable utilities explicitly with `--utilities`, independently of `--prepot`. Spellbook Swap completes before entry. The shared controller then summons after crossing while continuing the same cycle's movement/attack policy. Summoning adds no policy wait or attack delay; every in-arena cycle still counts toward simulation room time. Disabled controls retain their original setup timing. The integration suite uses both entrance barrier tiles and verifies that each thrall first spawns inside the arena, outside preparation consumes no summon runes, the agents move on their summon cycle without changing attack cooldown, both role summons deal damage, and native spell costs, expiry, Death Charge energy, live prep stats and unchanged human inventory remain covered. Earlier pre-entry utility runs left initial thralls outside the complete barrier; their timings and damage totals are historical results, not measurements of the corrected entry flow.

The simulation `--server-boosts` profile loads the actual existing LoginScript and StatRegenScript, matching live combat boosts and passive regeneration. Earlier potion-only profiles omit those scripts; their timings must remain separately labeled. A native regression verifies the shared profile script list against full Magic drain and recurring decay.
