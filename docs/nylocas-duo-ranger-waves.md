# Duo Nylocas ranger: priorities by last-spawned wave

**Wave 1: twisted bow west range small.**

Start from `(3292,4249)`, shoot the west small, then move east during cooldown. The normal recorded shot is `s1+1`. The mage handles south magic. This is the first action of the rotation; the detailed continuation follows below.

This guide supplies target ownership and recorded routes for the [strategy hierarchy](nylocas-duo-ranger-strategy.md). **Before wave 20, the throughput policy takes precedence over the recorded ordering and offsets below:** attack whenever ready, plan follow-ups during cooldown, and usually finish a damaged ranged small before opening a full-HP one. Wave 10's chin packet remains reserved; the wave-20 west flicker begins the later timed rotation. Other early bow/chin/scratch entries are flexible work, not deadlines. `sN` means the **actual** spawn tick of wave N, including stalls. “Wave N” in an entry means N was the last wave to spawn; “wave-X target” identifies that crab's birth wave. North/south distinguish horizontal-lane smalls; west/east distinguish south-lane smalls. Big anchors are southwest tiles and do not identify two separate bigs.

Timings are replay observation offsets, not additional delays to insert after seeing a spawn. “Immediate” attacks usually appear at birth+1 in Blert. Read current style before attacking flickers. A pending attack follows its target timer when the next wave appears; it is not cancelled or reset by a new wave. Flexible clearing follows the hierarchy and need not occur in exactly the same order in every recording.

Throughout all waves, apply the [prayer upkeep and protection policy](nylocas-duo-ranger-strategy.md#prayer-upkeep-and-protection): keep prayers active between attacks, restore with prayer potions when low, and protect against fighting nylocas hitting this player, prioritizing big attackers. Protection follows live threats rather than a fixed wave script and still covers old attackers ignored during final cleanup.

## Wave 2: twisted bow east range small

Move across toward `(3298,4249)` and bow the wave-2 east south-slot ranged small, normally `s2+3`. The opening bow's five-tick cooldown prevents a second immediate shot on a wave only four ticks later. Continue south; mage owns west magic. Take useful attacks while ready; the south packet does not reserve cooldown.

## Wave 3: move south and prepare the wave-3 ranged small

Move toward `(3295–3296,4243–4244)`. In the recordings there is usually no attack while wave 3 remains current, but the planner should fill available attack ticks: the next action is a black chin at the wave-3 south east-slot ranged small around its age four, when wave 4 has just spawned. A few recordings chin one tick earlier. Do not label this an immediate wave-3 prefire.

## Wave 4: chin wave-3 south range; scratch wave-2 south melee

Chin the arriving wave-3 south ranged small, usually `s4+0`, then ordinary claw scratch the wave-2 south west-slot melee small at `s4+3`. Stay around the south mouth. The mage handles the wave-4 south magic big. The recordings carry the wave-4 west ranged small into the wave-5 packet. Under the pre-cap policy, kill it sooner if useful; the wave-5 big is not a hard deadline.

## Wave 5: blowpipe west ranged big, then the recent west ranged small

Move west and open on the wave-5 west big from about `(3290,4248)`, commonly `s5+3`. Continue if needed, then clear the wave-4 west north-slot ranged small. Use the long interval before wave 6 for fresh ranged splits and useful Eye work, sometimes the wave-5 east magic small if the mage has not finished it. Move back toward south before wave 6/7. Neither the number of blowpipe hits on the big nor the split colors are fixed.

## Wave 6: south ranged small and useful follow-ups

Chin the wave-6 south west-slot ranged small as it comes down the lane, often `s6+1…3`; otherwise finish it with the blowpipe when it arrives. Fresh wave-5 split work may come first. Use free attack ticks, and stage at the south mouth during cooldown when it serves the next useful attack. The next south bow is flexible. The wave-6 east melee big is deliberately saved for the upcoming claw special.

## Wave 7: south ranged big, then claw special the wave-6 east melee big

Bow the wave-7 south ranged big promptly, usually `s7+1…4`; blowpipe is a recorded alternative when the prior cooldown or room state differs. Move toward the southeast/east side during cooldown and claw **special** the arriving wave-6 east melee big, usually `s7+6…9`. This is a consistently observed burst assignment. Nearby wave-7 melee scratching or south magic coverage is optional. End positioned for the east small, often near `(3301,4248)`.

## Wave 8: chin east ranged small when it remains available

Chin the wave-8 east south-slot ranged small promptly from the east side, or blowpipe it as it enters if the chin opportunity was missed. This chin is conditional, much less universal than wave 20 or 23. Finish useful fresh splits or the previous local packet if the partner already covered the lane target. Do not take over the wave-8 west magic big by default.

## Wave 9: finish east-side work, then blowpipe west ranged big

While still east, finish the wave-8 ranged small/splits and optionally Eye the wave-9 east magic small. Traverse back through center and blowpipe the wave-9 west ranged big as it reaches range, commonly around `s9+6…12`; blowpipe special is also used. This is not a universal birth-tick bow prefire. Recent ranged splits follow. Finish on the west/central line around `(3293,4248)` for wave 10.

## Wave 10: chin the ranged lanes, beginning west

Use black chins on the ranged groups. A common route is west around `s10+1`, east around `+4`, then south around `+7`, moving across the central row. It is not a mandatory three-throw script: target availability, mage coverage and remaining wave-9 splits change which throws are useful. A chin primary target may be either small in a pair; do not mistake that selection for evidence the other small was ignored. Some south throws happen once wave 11 is current.

## Wave 11: finish the wave-10 ranged workload

Continue the wave-10 south chin if pending, then blowpipe its remaining smalls, ranged splits and surviving east ranged big when useful. Favor damaged ranged finishes, then younger comparable smalls, over low-value near-expiry targets. Hold central tiles around `(3295,4248)`. The all-magic wave-11 arrivals are mainly the mage's packet; the ranger's recurrent off-style contribution is the south east-slot magic small, often attacked in wave 12.

## Wave 12: Eye recent magic targets; clear fresh ranged splits

Eye the wave-11 south east-slot magic small and fresh magic splits when free, with blowpipe on fresh ranged splits. There is no recurring ranger requirement to chase the wave-12 melee lane spawns. Move toward west/central north-row tiles `(3293–3295,4249)` for wave 13. Long-lasting melee crabs are precisely the kind of old targets that should not consume a useful ranged/magic cooldown.

## Wave 13: west magic/ranged pair, then south ranged small as it arrives

Eye the wave-13 west south-slot magic small when unclaimed, and blowpipe the west north-slot ranged small. The order can change with remaining splits and cooldown. The wave-13 south east-slot ranged small is also the ranger's work, but is very often finished in wave 14. Stay near west center initially and then move south; do not assume every target born in 13 must die before 14.

## Wave 14: south work, retaining the wave-13 south ranged target

Finish the wave-13 south ranged small and take the wave-14 south west-slot ranged small. Eye the wave-14 south east-slot magic small if available; this often extends into wave 15. The mage works on west magic. The recordings often defer the wave-14 east ranged big to waves 16–17. The current policy brings it forward: it becomes eligible at age 8 during wave-15 east-side work, needs priority by age 10, and has an age-16 finish goal. Keep it in the pending-work set until observed dead.

## Wave 15: east magic/ranged pair

Move east toward `(3300–3301,4248–4249)`, Eye the east north-slot magic small and blowpipe the east south-slot ranged small. Either can come first depending on unfinished south work and partner coverage. The wave-15 west south-slot ranged small is often finished later from center. Leave the south magic big to the mage's normal work. Work on the wave-14 east big while on this side; once attacked, finish it through misses and nonlethal hits. Avoid extending eastward travel after the east work is handled.

## Wave 16: finish the east packet and prepare the wave-16 flickers

Finish the wave-14 east ranged big if it survived its age-16 goal, ahead of ordinary small clearing. Complete remaining wave-15 east work when its finish priority permits. Take the wave-15 west ranged small when convenient during the return to center. A wave-16 west ranged small can be chinned early, but this is not universal. The main wave-16 packet is usually attacked **after wave 17 spawns**, once its three smalls have passed through the lane flickers and are ranged. Do not shoot their initial colors indiscriminately.

## Wave 17: blowpipe wave-16 ranged smalls; finish the deferred big

Finish the wave-14 east ranged big if still useful, then blowpipe the wave-16 east, south and west smalls when currently ranged, normally ages roughly 8–14. Their initial colors differ, so identify them by spawn lane/slot as well as current state. Order is flexible; recent useful splits can interleave. The newly arrived wave-17 magic bigs are predominantly the mage's work, not a fresh mandatory ranger big packet. Return toward `(3293,4248)`.

## Wave 18: clear smalls and hold the ranged bigs until safe

Keep clearing smalls while the three wave-18 ranged bigs enter. Never attack these bigs in the lanes, even from bow range; verify their observed anchors are inside the room. Normally wait for the wave-19 lane bigs to flicker melee at `s19+5` before starting their kills. The **west** wave-18 big may be killed earlier only after entering and only when there are no small nylos left, including dying bodies. South/east have no early exception. Their final mage change at age 26 is the finish deadline, not their brief age-5 mage flicker.

## Wave 19: release wave-18 ranged bigs at the melee flicker; west mage is fallback

Use the actual wave-19 spawn, including any stalls. At `s19+5`, the lane bigs flicker melee: use this as the guide to kill the in-room wave-18 ranged bigs, generally from west/center. This late kill timing aims to defer their split births until after wave 20 is admitted. Keep attacking on cooldown and prefer finishing damaged ranged bigs; partner claims still apply.

Only Eye the shared wave-19 west mage if there is no other useful attack. The partner helps with it and handles complementary mage bigs. A hit on west does not commit the ranger to finishing it over ranged or small work. The team objective is all three wave-18 bigs, all three wave-19 bigs and two of the wave-20 bigs, with **zero wave-20 retries**. Do not count dying bodies as free cap slots.

## Wave 20: drop surviving pre-cap bigs and reserve the west ranged flicker

As soon as wave 20 actually spawns, leave every surviving big born before wave 20 alive, including previously hit wave-14/18/19 bigs. Discard pending pre-cap work and use the post-cap rotation. New children have their own birth wave and remain eligible for clearing.

The wave-20 west big **starts melee**. Twisted bow it during its ranged flicker, typically recorded at `s20+6` (requested at age five). The partner handles south; leave east alive for the two-of-three team policy. After the west shot, clear fresh ranged splits and reposition east for wave 21. `(3293,4248)` remains the usual west/central base. The old cap applies to admitting wave 20; the larger admission cap starts at wave 21.

## Wave 21: immediate east chin, then fresh ranged splits

Chin the east ranged pair around `s21+1`, commonly from `(3301,4248)` or its neighbors. Either east primary target is acceptable if the splash covers the pair. Return toward center and clear fresh ranged splits, especially from the recent big kills; take Eye work only when it fits. Do not replace this first throw with a long-cooldown attack on an old crab.

## Wave 22: fresh split clearing and south-prefire preparation

Mostly blowpipe young ranged splits from waves 20/21 and the current births; Eye young magic if needed. Finish surviving wave-21 east ranged work when useful. The ranger does not have a universal wave-22 big prefire. Move to south range and reserve the bow for wave 23. Do not start a late Eye, claw or bow that leaves the south prefire unavailable.

## Wave 23: immediate south big bow; west north ranged flicker

Twisted bow the south ranged big promptly, normally `s23+1`, from roughly `(3295,4243–4245)`. During its five-tick cooldown move west to `(3291,4248)`. Blowpipe the west **north-slot** small at `s23+6`, when it has flickered from magic to ranged. Reserve the following attack for the west south-slot small as it returns ranged at `s23+8`. That second small is usually a **wave-24-current** action, not a missing wave-23 task.

## Wave 24: finish wave-23 west south, then kill wave-24 west big early

Blowpipe the wave-23 west south-slot small at `s23+8`, usually `s24+0`. From the west mouth, kill the new wave-24 west big before its initial ranged window closes: commonly blowpipe `s24+4`, or bow `s24+2` as an alternative. If it survives into magic, an Eye attack around `s24+6` is recorded. Clear fresh ranged splits when that packet permits, then return toward center. The south/east bigs are complementary mage work by default.

## Wave 25: young ranged splits, then the south ranged big

Blowpipe fresh ranged splits from the wave-23/24 kills and new births. Work on the wave-25 south ranged big once useful; it often survives into wave 26 or 27 before the ranger attacks/finishes it. Do not manufacture a mandatory birth-tick bow here. Hold central tiles around `(3295,4248)` and use Eye for fresh unclaimed magic when appropriate.

## Wave 26: continue split clearing and wave-25 south big

Only a short natural interval separates waves 26 and 27. Continue the recent ranged splits, finish the wave-25 south big if it is the useful remaining ranged work, or Eye a fresh magic split. There is no fixed requirement to attack a new wave-26 lane big. Avoid an unnecessary long attack that consumes the coming south flicker window.

## Wave 27: clear young splits; stage for its south big's ranged form

Finish useful wave-25 south-big work and clear fresh splits. The new wave-27 south big starts melee, flickers through magic, then becomes ranged; the ranger commonly blowpipes it at `s27+8`. With an unstalled eight-tick interval, that attack occurs **as wave 28 spawns**. Stage around `(3296,4246)` rather than describing it as an immediate wave-27 green prefire.

## Wave 28: wave-27 south big, then the wave-28 south flickers

Blowpipe the wave-27 south big when its ranged window is due. From south-center, blowpipe wave-28 south **east-slot** small when ranged around `s28+6`, then south **west-slot** small around `s28+8`. Clear young ranged splits during any retries, with the goal of admitting 29 quickly. Two or three wave-28 stalls can be acceptable; do not chase old melee to force an arbitrary zero-stall target. If 29 has already spawned before either southern attack, carry it into the next entry while the target is still fresh and actionable.

## Wave 29: finish fresh south work and prepare the west pair

Finish the wave-28 south flicker packet if pending, and clear recent ranged splits while moving west/central. Preserve the **wave-29** west pair: south-slot small while currently ranged, north-slot small when it becomes ranged at `s29+6`, then south-slot again at `+8` if still alive/ranged. A common south-slot shot is `+4` or `+8`; north `+6` is especially stable. If wave 30 arrives after four ticks, the north shot belongs to the wave-30-current interval. This is why “ignore ≤29 immediately on spawning 29” would incorrectly erase a recurring packet.

## Wave 30: pending wave-29 west flickers, then wave-30 west big and south chin

Honor the wave-29 west north shot if now due and its surviving companion when ranged. Kill the new wave-30 west ranged big early, commonly with a blowpipe at `s30+4`; finish with another ranged attack if needed. Chin the wave-30 south **east-slot** small on its ranged flicker at `s30+6`. The south west-slot small starts magic in the recordings; the published guide table has a known error here. With no stall before wave 31, both the `+4` big shot and `+6` chin occur once 31 is current. Keep them pending across that transition.

## Wave 31: finish the lane rotation, then apply the cleanup cutoff

First finish the wave-30 west big / south chin if their windows are due. For the wave-31 west pair, blowpipe the north-slot small on its ranged flicker around `s31+6` and the south-slot small while ranged, commonly `+4` or `+8`. Chin the south ranged flickers around `s31+8…10`, choosing a currently matching target that gives useful splash. Blowpipe/chin the wave-31 east ranged target as it becomes reachable, most commonly around `+13` but with frequent later finishes. Avoid a rigid order if damage rolls, partner coverage or prior stalls changed what is still alive.

Once that last lane packet is complete or no longer actionable, enter **final cleanup**: ignore survivors born in waves ≤29, prioritize remaining young ranged targets born in wave 30+, Eye young magic, and leave complementary melee work to the mage unless useful nearby filler is needed. New children from an old parent have their own birth wave and must be reconsidered. Follow young splits northward as necessary; stop chasing ignored old crabs. Some human recordings attack an old survivor even here; that is a recorded deviation from the agreed basic cutoff, not an extra mandatory step.
