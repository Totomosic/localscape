-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wave 6 stall (These are rare but are avoidable if they are going to happen)

Start pathing away from the wave 5 range big to avoid travel time on the sang you do after

After killing the wave 5 range big fill ticks on a mage depending on how fast you killed your range big compared to how fast the mager killed thier mage big.
Priority sangs 
Wave 3 (aggro) > Wave 5 > Wave 2 melee (aggro) 
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wave 8 Stall (This is rare but more common than the 6 stall by alot)

There are two stages to a wave 8 stall
Less severe (hit more smalls)
more severe (Delay the claw spec)

In less severe cases of stalling its probably a sign that in general you are going to your wave 6/7 south rangers too early. You should hit splits until the wave 6/7 are BOTH going to be in BP range.
Priorities are...
Rangers > Sangs > Melees on the south side of the room

In more severe cases of stalling wave 8 it means you are already correctly hitting splits and waiting for the 6/7 to both be in blowpipe range and wave 8 still stalled.
This can be avoid by delaying when you claw spec the wave 6 big melee as stalling wave 8 in these instances is usually caused by the melee splitting before the wave 9 check.
To accomplish this...
Understand first that you and your partner didnt hit well on splits prior to u claw speccing your big
(If Killing your wave 7 range big is fast) 2 pipes or less
Delay popping your big by adding a 4 tick action between killing your Wave 7 range big and claw speccing your melee wave 6 big 
(If Killing your wave 7 Range big is slow) *3 pipes or more
You can immediatley claw spec your wave 6 melee big if killing the wave 7 is slow
