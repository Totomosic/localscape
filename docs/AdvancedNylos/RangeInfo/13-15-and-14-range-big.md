--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wave 13 spawn without 12 dinhs

Make sure you are always swapping to bp after you scythe wave 12
DO NOT! Sang > Bp For this wave or 14 or 15. Always Bp > Sang as it is more tick efficient to play off of.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wave 13 Spawn With 12 dinhs

Assuming your not stuck on rangers when 13 spawns which would take priority you should...
be swapping to sang staff and be ready to 1tick and mages that spawn from the 11 big (because the mager will null these). If Wave 13 spawns first before the splits you should sang that instead. Either way you should be ready to sang one of these two options

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wave 14 spawn with 12 dinhs

Depending on splits before 14 spawns you will either start wave 14 south with a sang or bp. Recognizing that you can bp first than sang will be efficient more maximizing ticks and minimizing stalls
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wave 15 Spawn With 12 dinhs

If you end wave 14 on a sang you will start 15 with a bp
If you end wave 14 on a bp you will start wave 15 on a sang
assuming you have no range splits to play off of between the south and east lane

14 range big
