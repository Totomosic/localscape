w31 North mages

Its important to understand when to kill this as mage vs range.

To do this you should first know your expiry in the moment and what cleanup you need to have to meet it.

lets pretend we have 243 wave 31 spawn again. So in this case 233 29 spawn equates to 304.8 cleanup. 

The formula is:
wave 31 spawn + 15.6 = x and is x greater or less than my expiry

if x is less than your time you need to meet for cleanup than it needs to die as range and if x is greater than or equal to your time you need to meet for cleanup than it needs to die as mage.
Example 1: 233 wave 29 spawn / 250 wave 31 spawn = kill as mage
Example 2: 233 wave 29 spawn / 245 Wave 31 spawn = kill as range

it helps to know what time you need to meet for multiple different expiries and what that equates to for cleanup
