25 Ranged Big

The 25 Ranged big is dependent on roomstate and that roomstate is mostly dependent on doing transition correctly and prepping your mages dinhs correctly.
⁠19-20-aka-transition ⁠20-21-tbow-3bp-chin-old-method  ⁠22-west-prefire
Assuming you do all that correctly every time popping the 25 big should be free almost everytime and simple to decide.

You should pop the 25 Ranged big when you have 2-3 range smalls alive. This Will minimize your time sanging as you will optimize feeding yourself staggered range smalls.
Waiting to kill your 25 ranged big until you have zero rangers left will result in you sanging more.

If you are overwhelmed with rangers you should ignore the 2-3 rule and just pop the 25 regardless once 27 spawns as most old smalls in the room will be expired between 26-27.
Making sure you pop the south 25 every single time will result in a better cleanup, just be aware of roomstate and that you will not always have a static spot to pop the 25 ranged big.

Extra Mage bigs

Extra mage bigs are risky and are extremely roomstate dependent. In most scenerios you will not sang any mage bigs 25-27.
The general goal of the ranger is to push the waves, popping an extra mage big is more of a luxury than a standard so avoid this if you are not very comfortable with roomstates in general.
