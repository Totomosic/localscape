Mini-Simple Prayer Guide


 It is possible to be more max eff with prayers than this, this is just meant to be something simple that will consistently work without the use of blowpipe specs 

Waves 1-9 (Camp MELEE or MAGE or no prayer) your only really ever praying against the wave 2 or 3 crabs.

Waves 10-13 (after your 2nd chin pray MAGE) specifically after your 2nd chin so you can react to Chin > bp > chin > chin

Waves 14-19 (pray RANGE during your sang on the bp sang south prefire)

Waves 20-25 (pray MELEE during your wave 20 tbow)

Waves 26-28 (pray RANGE when 26 spawns)

Waves 28-31 (Pray MAGE during your 28 bp flickers)
