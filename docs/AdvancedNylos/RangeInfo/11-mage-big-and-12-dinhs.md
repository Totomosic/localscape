This Channel Will teach you how to pop the wave 11 mage big with and without the 12 dinhs setup.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Universal Queue

The objective is to pop this so that it splits after wave 13 spawns regardless of dinhs or no dinhs. You can time this by popping the wave 11 mage big any time after wave 12 spawns.

Roomstate dependent opportunities
You may pop this early if the roomstate is good however and there is no risk of stalling wave 12 by doing so
A good roomstate = 
A very good wave 10
And The mage had a very good wave 11

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No Wave 12 Dinhs 

Prioritize all smalls range smalls before hitting the wave 11 big
Prioritize all mage smalls s10 or higher before hitting the wave 11 big. This will result in sometimes not hitting the wave 11 big which is fine.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wave 12 Dinhs

-Before Wave 12 spawns prioritize popping all rangers and then mages s10 or higher 

-Once Wave 12 spawns stand somewhere in the middle of the room to pop the mage big but on the northern line parallel to the barrier
