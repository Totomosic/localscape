This Channel will talk about how to Maximize your efficiency waves 9-11 and avoid...
Wave 10 stalls
Wave 11 stalls

This channel will start from blowpiping the wave 8 east small ranger.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wave 9 Setup

-The priority is to so kill the wave 9 range big asap without losing ticks
-After piping the wave 8 small you should hit rangers if rangers spawned until you get in blowpipe range of the wave 9 west range big
 -If there arent enough rangers to get to the big without losing ticks than you fill with a sang on the Wave west range small
-Any time you sang during wave 9 you always have enough time to path to the big without losing ticks and should be what you do.

NOTE: Popping the wave 9 range big ASAP is important for avoiding wave 10 stalls specifically as a ranger spawning from this is important.

After killing the big you should hit whatever you can while managing to be on time to...
First tick chinning Wave 10
or
3rd/ 2nd tick chinning Wave 10
Do not 4th tick chin! as you need to stand on a completely different tile that is inconvenient for it to register before the wave 11 check. 

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wave 10 Spawn / Avoid Wave 10 stall 


1st tick
-In scenerios where you first tick wave 10 you have time to blowpipe anything in the room before you throw the second chin. 
-This blowpipe will register before the wave 11 check and will drastically cut down on wave 10 stalls

This looks like...
-Chin > Blowpipe > Chin > Chin

3rd/2nd tick
-This is basically the same thing but the blowpipe happened before you chinned and you blowpiped the same tick or 1 tick before wave 10 spawned

This looks like... 
-Blowpipe > Chin > Chin > Chin 
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wave 10 Setup into Wave 11 / Avoid Wave 11 stall
After Chinning West 10 you should pay attention if you 1shot it.
If you dont 1 shot it you should rechin east if nothing died
If you only killed 1 crab you should chin south to get a head start on not stalling wave 11 and killing all the range smalls
if you kill both crabs than you should usually be clear to spawn 11 and should chin east next to get a head start on splits.

If The Big wave 10 ranger is alive after chinning it make sure you pop all range smalls before popping the big. This will help maximize your chances of not stalling wave 11
