Transition into postcap is one of if not the most important part of getting a clear and fast post cap.

When we are talking about a good transition that means the amount of bigs we kill from waves 18, 19, and 20. The goal is to kill all 18s, all 19s, and all 20s. Do not dwell on killing bigs after 20 spawns except for the two 20s. There are two exceptions for mage which can be found in [19-21](../MageInfo/19-21-3t-mage-weapon.md)

Mage [19-21](../MageInfo/19-21-3t-mage-weapon.md)

Range [19-21](../RangeInfo/19-20-aka-transition.md)

There are subtle differences in how you play in post cap as well based on how well you hit after transition as well as how good your transition was. A good transition can carry bad hits and good hits can carry a bad transition. Playing for a good transition will often result in the best odds of a smooth post cap.

More specifically when we are talking about transition bigs we are talking about ones that split before the wave 22 dim and any that split after will contribute to a wave 28 stall.
