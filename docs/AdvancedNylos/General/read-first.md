The purpose of This discord isn't to be a general/beginners nylo guide but instead to be a stepping stone into faster averages for your nylo waves all the way into the  30x territory. I will cover common struggle points for magers and rangers so it is expected that you fully know your rotation by this point on either a basic or intermediate level.

If you aren't at the point of consistent 319 or better i would recommend spending more time on the flash nylo guide or copying various vods before applying the information in these guides.

In addition advanced Nylo information is just not a publicly available source of information and people are often limited unless they know exactly who to talk to.

My goals for this discord is to teach:
better rotations / tick optimization
roomstate awareness
knowledge of expiries (important moments where a bunch of nylos expire)

This discord is not in a final product state and it will probably never be in a final product state. If you are in this discord and there is something i have not talked about that you think should be its own channel feel free to talk to me about it. Since this is an advanced nylo guide i skip multiple waves that are not common struggle points or topics that i deem too simple for this discord. If its not talked about in this discord than i prefer that you learn the information before applying the new information in this discord for ease of learning and/or teaching.
