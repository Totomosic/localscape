This channel explains the purpose of dimming cleanup as well as the various purposes of the multiple cleanup dim options

In duo nylos we dim/darken using the nyloer plugin on wave 29. When we dim we are calling this our EXPIRY.

What that means is... 

Once wave 29 spawns the goal is to kill every nylo in the room within 31.2 seconds before the wave 29 crabs automatically expire. So if wave 29 spawns at 233 room time then wave 29 expires at 304.8 and this equates to a 314.4 boss spawn. In short someone would simply call that a "314 expiry" to summarize all that.

The more 28 stalls you have before your 29 spawn, the more likely you are to meet your expiry. The more experienced you are the less 28 stalls you can have for your dim/darken.

in order of likeliness of meeting your expiry you should...
5 stall 28 (learner) This is the slowest id reccomend for overall raids
4 stall 28 (intermediate)
3 stall 28 (advanced) This is the fastest id reccomend for overall raids
2 stall 28 (advanced / nylo reset)
1 stall 28 (advanced / nylo reset )

27/x dim and  28/x dim

These are our two essentially normal cleanup dims.
it is important to the average of your nylos to choose the appropriate cleanup dim for your skill level.
your cleanup dim of choice effects the entirety of which dim preset you choose, it is the MOST important dim.
⁠4-dims-for-different-skill-leve… 

if the 27 dim is confusing, the purpose of this is to just count 27 stalls as equivilent to 28 stalls.
EX: for 28 12 the normality is to have (28 28) stalls than 29 spawns.  However if you have (27 28) than 29 spawns than 28 12 will add an unnessecary 4 extra ticks before dimming. 27/20 would fix this by count the (27 28) stalls as being equivilent to (28 28) stalls. 

The equivilent for 27/x : 28/x is
27/x = 28/x + 8 = dim 
EX: 27/20= 28/12 + 8 = 27/20 
wave 27 is naturally 8 ticks long
 
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
29 dim
29 dim is another cleanup dim
you should have 29 dim plus a 28/x or 27/x dim

why have two cleanup dims?

28/x and 27/x are your normal cleanup dims
29 dim covers the abnormal situation of insta 29s
insta 29 means there are no 27 or 28 stalls before 29 spawns.
