(Pre-Cap)
----------------------------------

Normal stalls
-------------------
There are zero intentional stalls in precap.

Rng Stalls
-------------------
It is normal to stall between waves 10-16 to an extent. It is normal to average 2-3 stalls every precap with 1 being an outlier and 4 or less being outliers. However on very rare occasions you can stall wave 6 without a mistake and wave 17.

Accidental Stalls
-------------------
Any stall before wave 10 is almost always a result of a mistake.
It is normal to stall between waves 10-16 to an extent. It is normal to average 2-3 stalls every precap with 1 being an outlier and 4 or less being outliers.
A wave 17 stall is almost always a mistake.
A wave 18 and 19 stall are always a result of a mistake
 
(Post-Cap)
----------------------------------

Normal Stalls
---------------------
It is intentional to stall 28 3 times in overalls. (These can be extended to 3 29 stalls on insta 29)
It is normal to stall 29 but not too many times, the amount of stalls you have here must appropriately reflect the amount of bigs you popped.
It is normal to stall 29 but not too many times, the amount of stalls you have here must appropriately reflect the amount of bigs you popped.
To elaborate on this, if you pop 1-2 bigs you should only have like 1-2 stalls usually after 29 spawns. If you pop 3 bigs you should have more stalls. And if you pop 4 you should have even more stalls. There is a ratio where the benefit of  (stalls : bigs killed) is a positive vs a negative. Meaning if you killed 4 bigs but had 7 29-30 stalls you most likely popped too much. Or if you didnt have enough 28 stalls that most likely means that you didnt pop enough. The channel guide will link to sections on why this is important and how to accomplish this.

Rng Stalls
---------------------
It is Very rare to stall 20-27 because of Rng, however it does happen on occasion.
Stalling 28 more than 3 times without making a mistake is rare but does happen.

Accidental stalls
---------------------
-Almost all the time a 20-27 stall is a reflection of mistakes. 
-More than 3 28 stalls is a mistake unless you aren't going for max eff nylo times.
-Stalling 29-30 too many times is a sign of a mistake.
