Its important to understand when to kill this as mage vs range.

There's two ways of doing this, either way involves some quick on the fly simple math, but requires you to pay attention to your room timer and when you dimmed.

Take your expiry (233 or 314 expiry will be our example), this is when you spawned wave 29 as a reminder.
Take your final wave spawn (243 will be our example).
Subtract them from each other.
then if the number is smaller than 15.6 you have to kill it while mage (preferably the mager kills it) and if its greater than 15.6 you kill it while its range. 

This is Expiry - w31 spawn = x - 31.2 = y

So in our example for a 233 wave 29 spawn aka 314 expiry you would kill the w31 when it turned range.
If the end wave spawn was 250 then someone would have to kill the mage before it turned range
