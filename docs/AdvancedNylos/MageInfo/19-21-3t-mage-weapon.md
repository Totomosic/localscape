This channel will talk about the 19-21 rotation

This is a big 28 tick rotation that can be split up into two chunks
19-20 which is 12 ticks
and 20-21 which is 16 ticks

If you ever lose ticks always readjust so that you barrage 21 west double mages on the 1st or 2nd tick.

do NOT prefire the 19 east with 3t mage weapon unless it is 2ticks late or (3tick late with thrall out)
this will cause it to split before 20 spawns

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scythe 20 Before 21 rotation

it is important you do NOT prefire wave 20 south unless there are no immediate splits or 19mage bigs to hit.
It is more consistent to kill it melee via a scythe or in some cases macs

it is HIGHLY important when you are hitting mage splits in these rotations that you are maging south and
it is HIGHLY important when you are hitting melee splits in these rotations that you are meleeing north

(Wave 19 spawns)
Shadow 19 east mage > dart 18 north ranger > fill ticks by hitting 19 mage bigs, mage splits, or melee splits > Melee the 20 south big...
dont prefire wave 20 south mage big as a reminder 
if you dont shadow wave 19 east mage big on the first tick than you can skip the dart

From Meleeing the 20 south big the next action after that depends on what tick and what weapon you used to melee the 20 south big
First tick scythe: Scythe 20 > ayak a split > Barrage 21 > Melee north split
Second tick scythe: Scythe 20 > dart or afk 2 ticks >Barrage 21 > Melee north split (skip the dart if it would drag your away from the barrier before the barrage)
Third tick scythe: > Scythe 20 > afk 1 tick > Barrage 21 > Melee north split 
Fourth tick scythe > Scythe 20 > Barrage 21 > Melee North split
Fifth tick sulphur > Sulphur 20 > Barrage 21 > Melee North split 
Second Tick Sulphur > Sulphur 20 > ayak a split > Barrage 21 > Melee north split

Make sure you are always hitting a melee small immediatley after barraging 21 on the 1st tick
this melee will die before the 22 check
don't melee after barraging 21 if you barrage 21 on the second tick
this only works with weapons that have overkill such as sulphur blades or macs

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scythe 20 after 21 rotations

Sometimes the room is too full and you arent killing enough smalls before the 21 barrage so you will have to hold the 20 big until after the 21 barrage.
this is entirely roomstate dependent so make your best judgement based on amount of bigs killed and amount of smalls being killed
make sure if you hold the big until after 21 barrage that you immediately scythe it after barraging.
you cannot do anything in between the two actions or it will change to ranged

Here are some rotation examples of holding the 20 until after the barrage
everything will end on (barrage 21 > scythe 20 melee big)
you can also do these rotations every time and it wont be horrible but it wont be 100% optimal. However it is much better than getting lost.
(wave 20 spawns) 16 ticks 
3t staff x5 > afk 1t > barrage 21 > scythe 20
3t staff x4 > mac/sulphur > barrage 21 >scythe 20
if you get lost you can tell wave 21 will spawn by when the 20 east melee big gets to the pillar
you can get one hit in once its at the pillar or right before the pillar
