This channel is about the wave 26 dinhs and how to perform it / setup for it
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose

The 26 dinhs has two purposes

The primary purpose of the 26 dinhs is to push 2 stall 28 into wave 29 spawn if your a more experienced player or 3 stall 28 into wave 29 spawn if your a less experienced player

The secondary purpose of the 26 dinhs is to give you splits early into cleanup
these splits will not be part of cleanup if you stall4 or more 28s
If you stall 4 or more 28s after wave 26 that is a sign you messed up

common mistakes for not pushing 29 fast enough will be talked about in ⁠26-29-complex-roomstate-awarene…
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Setup

I will discuss the setup very quickly As this is largely roomstate dependent. 
I will discuss a simple general version here and the more complex version will be in ⁠26-29-complex-roomstate-awarene… 

In general you will want to kill the west 27 mage big in lane
this will split into your 26 dinhs
Killing it out of lane is debatable and entirely dependent on roomstate 

after killing however many bigs you decided to kill you want to prioritize smalls in this order of priority while waiting for the wave 28 small melee to be in range.
North Melees > South Mages > North Mages

Make sure when you hit smalls you are clicking s27s or higher

Performing the 26 dinhs

You are always dinhs off the 26 big, you want your main dinhs hitsplat on the big
depending on if theres any melee crabs on the southeast pillar or not you will dinhs the 26 from either
The north facing side of the big
The east facing side of the big

you want your exp drop to go off the tick the wave 28s on the west and east side line up with the barrier
Playing with true tile on your melee nylos is an option to make this easier to see
If you should choose that option you click the 26 big when the true tile for the west crabs are directly on the barrier

The other method for timing the 26 dinhs is with an instance timer.
you want to sync a 4 tick metronome with your nylo instance timer before starting the room
You simply click the 26 big when the 28s are entering the room and the tick counter says 1 on it.

Post 26 dinhs small rotation

After the 26 dinhs you should always
hit the east melee small w28 or Hit a big in the room to get splits faster if the dinhs was VERY good
You can see it in the screenshot above. This small is fixed and will always be here.
Hitting a big when the dinhs is average to sub par will clutter post 29 and cause alot of 29-30 stalls 

After hitting 1 crab and only 1 crab go afk for 1 tick in front of the 26s corpse if it died and be ready to 1 tick whatever split comes out of it
Prioritize melee gear and 2 way into mage to react to mages
(or) if the 26 melee big didnt die ,be aware of that, be in front of the 23 bigs corpse, which is splitting 2 ticks after the 26 would split
