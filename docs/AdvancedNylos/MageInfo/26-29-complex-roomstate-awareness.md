General Info 
In General the goal for pushing 29 is with 0 stalls 20-27 and 2 28 stalls or 3 28 stalls if your newer.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
24-26 Observational Period
It is Important to use scything 24s and 25 and Maging the 26 aggro as an observational period to scan the room and plan your next move.

Here you are deciding whether the roomstate is GOOD or BAD based on a number of factors

Good Room state Observations
killed a lot of bigs on transition
a lot of south melees for the 26 dinhs
1 shotting your bigs 24-26
a lot of rangers spawned ( you may notice a general empty room more than you notice the actual rangers themselves)
killed 1 - 2 smalls between your bigs 24-26
Valuable barrages mixed with bigs or not mixed with bigs (see ⁠wave-25 to understand why barraging two s25 s24 or s26 is not a good barrage on its own)

Bad Room state Observations
didn't kill a lot of bigs on transition
Single Mage heavy room
Getting behind on your bigs 24-26
Not alot of rangers (Alot of smalls in the room)
No time to hit 1-2 smalls between bigs

An Average roomstate will often be a 50/50 mix of bad an good observations
The following will all be between 26 and 27
