Wave 9 and 10 and some 12 dinhs prep

There are two things you need to immediatley look at for after you kill your wave 8 big

You need to be in mage gear still and ready to mage splits. You want to clear approximately 2 mage smalls now before wave 10 spawns.
The reason you want to mage now is because when wave 10 spawns you dont want to be maging unless it can be in melee distance as it will not register before the wave 11 check most likely.

The other priority is killing any small that lands on the eastward facing side of the Southwest pillar.
This will prep your 12 dinhs properly so that you dont hit the big when you do a wave 12 dinhs.
You may have to continuously look out for this on waves 10/11/ and 12.

Additionally you should always make sure your targeting the newest spawns here regardless of if they are mage or melee
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11 Spawn and how to pop the 11 big

If you can 1st tick wave 11 this is good as it will clear in time before the 12 check
2nd tick has to be against the barrier for it to register in time

DONT Barrage east > Sang big 11 > Barrage south if the room is full.
If you stall wave 12 when you do this it will cause a chain stall
instead Barrage east > Barrage south > Sang 11
Additionally you can (Barrage east > Mac > Barrage south) This will maximize your chances of not stalling 11 if you are behind.

The general rule for safety of not stalling wave 12 is...
Dont sang the 11 big until wave 12 has spawned

You can prioritize smalls if your ranger has plenty of time to kill the big
If you prioritize smalls prioritize (East Melees > Southwest pillar on the eastward facing side mages/melees > Mages in general)

Approach whether to hit the big or smalls as a variable roomstate to roomstate, you will not make the same choice everytime.

12 Dinhs
When You Scythe the east wave 12 melee doubles make sure you prioritize the one on the south side
This will put your big scythe hit splat on the one that doesnt follow you after the scythe
The north melee should have the small hitsplat as it follows you and is part of the dinhs

after scything the 12 dinhs you should diagonally path north before clicking the 12 aggro on the west side
this movement avoids unessecarily popping the wave 12 big when the room is full 
if the room isnt full you can skip this movement to pop the 12 big

https://i.imgur.com/LvQFT1Z.png

If you are scything the doubles make sure your are scything first tick they are in melee range
Scything the tick after they are in melee range will mean that the following 12 dinhs does not register before the 14 check

If you are going to end up being a tick late because you had to hit something before the scythe that resulted in this than you should mac the south crab instead of scything it.
This will make sure you line up on tick for the 12 dinhs

After 12 dinhs
Imediately after the 12 dinhs you should either...
hit the south 13 melee or
 hit the west 14 mage

w12 melees and s13 melees are the lowest value as they will expire between 18 and 19 on a zero stall pre

Make sure you do not prioritize the 13 west mage aggro above 14s or 15s as leaving 14s or 15s can lead to wave 18 stalls more often
this is a good option to hit before 14 spawns

Ignore "s14" mage splits as these will be nulled if the 12 dinhs was performed correctly by both players
The ranger can handle these if they need to hit something
if you notice they werent nulled, feel free to hit them
