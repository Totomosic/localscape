# Range strategy

## Gear setup

### Melee gear

Void range set, Dizana's quiver, Necklace of anguish, dragon arrows, avernic treads (max), lightbearer, dragon claws

### Range gear

Void range set, Dizana's quiver, Necklace of anguish, dragon arrows, avernic treads (max), lightbearer, toxic blowpipe

### Mage gear

Void range set, Dizana's quiver, Necklace of anguish, dragon arrows, avernic treads (max), lightbearer, eye of ayak

### Additional items

- Twisted bow: used for longer range and better kill odds

- Black chinchompas: used for killing clumps of nylocas

## Basic overview

Prioritise nylocas in this order: range, mage, melee. In pre-cap you are assigned to kill the following bigs: wave 5 west big, wave 7 south big, wave 6 east big (claw special attack this), wave 9 west big, wave 10 east big, wave 14 east big, all 18 bigs, backup any 19 bigs, west wave 20 big (twisted bow as it flickers range).

Try not to lose any ticks, quickly swap between blowpipe and eye of ayak to achieve this. When bigs die, be ready to react to any new splits that come out and immediately attack them

## Key waves

- Wave 10: be early to throw chinchompas in all 3 lanes, West -> East -> South.

- Wave 20: twisted bow the 20 west as it flicks range then 3 blowpipes -> chin 21 east first tick.

- Wave 27: blowpipe the wave 27 south big as soon as flicks to range in the room.

- Wave 28-30: prefire wave 28 flickering nylos south, wave 29 flickering nylos west, blowpipe the 30 big when it is in range and then chin the 30s south when they flick range.
